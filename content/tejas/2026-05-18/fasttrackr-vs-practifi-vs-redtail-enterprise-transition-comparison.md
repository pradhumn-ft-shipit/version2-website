## A CRM and a transition tool do different jobs

A CRM holds the client relationship before, during and after a move. A transition tool does the move itself: working out which custodian and firm forms each account needs, filling them, getting them signed and following each account until it's done. Most firms moving books will use both. FastTrackr works with Wealthbox and Salesforce and can send data back to them.

## Questions to ask any vendor

- Which custodians are live today, and which are still coming?
- How does the paperwork go out: e-signature, filled PDFs for a wet signature, or the counterparty's own template?
- What happens after the client signs? Who follows NIGO rejections and status updates until each account is done?
- What can you start from? In a breakaway the old firm doesn't hand over an export, so it depends on your data access rights. In an acquisition the old firm hands over its exports.
- Is it software you run, a team that runs it for you, or both?

## FAQs

### Do I need a transition tool if I already have a CRM?

A CRM keeps the client record. It doesn't fill custodian and firm paperwork for every account or follow NIGO rejections after signing. That's the job a transition tool does, and the two can run side by side.
