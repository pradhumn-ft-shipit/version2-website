## Questions to ask any vendor

- Which custodians are live today, and which are still coming?
- How does the paperwork go out: e-signature, filled PDFs for a wet signature, or the counterparty's own template?
- What happens after the client signs? Who follows NIGO rejections and status updates until each account is done?
- What can you start from? In a breakaway the old firm doesn't hand over an export, so it depends on your data access rights. In an acquisition the old firm hands over its exports.
- Is it software you run, a team that runs it for you, or both?
